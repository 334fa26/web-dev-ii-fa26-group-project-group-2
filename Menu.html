Good to Know

A few things you may be wondering.

Do you offer gluten free options?   +

We bake a rotating selection of flourless treats, but our kitchen handles wheat flour daily and is not certified gluten-free.

How far ahead should I place a custom order?   +

We recommend at least 7 days for celebration cakes and 3 days for pastry boxes. Larger events may require more notice.

Do you deliver?   +

Local delivery is available for custom orders over $75 within 8 miles. A delivery fee is calculated with your quote.
